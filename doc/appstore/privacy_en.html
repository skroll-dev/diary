2. Overview and Scope

This privacy policy informs you about the nature, scope, and purpose of the processing of personal data within the mobile application “Mein KI-Tagebuch” (My AI Diary) (hereinafter “App”), as well as about your rights.

The App allows you to speak freely into your smartphone. Your spoken input is transcribed by artificial intelligence and processed into a structured diary entry. Protecting your very personal content is a core principle of our technical architecture (“Privacy by Design”).

3. What data we process

  • Account and master data: account identifier (User ID), authentication data
  • Voice input (audio): your spoken recordings made while creating an entry
  • Diary content: transcribed text, generated entry, mood tags and scores
  • Usage and device data: app version, device/operating system type, error/crash reports, timestamps
  • Subscription data: subscription status (active/inactive) – payment itself is processed via the App Store / Google Play

4. Voice Input and Zero-Disk Policy

Zero-Disk Policy: your audio recordings are never permanently stored on our servers or hard drives. The audio is processed exclusively in working memory (RAM) and is completely and irrevocably discarded immediately after the text entry has been created.

Only the result of the processing is stored – i.e. the diary entry you approved in text form, along with associated metadata (e.g. mood tag). The original audio file never leaves this in-memory process.

5. Special Categories of Personal Data (Art. 9 GDPR)

By their nature, diary entries may contain very personal information – such as details about health, religious or philosophical beliefs, political opinions, or sex life. Such data is considered a special category of personal data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR and enjoys special protection.

The processing of this content takes place exclusively on the basis of your explicit consent pursuant to Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. We obtain this consent separately within the App before the first use of the AI functionality. You may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

6. Processing by Artificial Intelligence

To provide the core service, we use the following AI services:

  • Speech recognition (transcription): Google Cloud Speech-to-Text (Chirp) converts your spoken input into text.
  • Text structuring: the multimodal model Gemini 2.5 Flash (Google Cloud Vertex AI) forms a coherent entry from what was spoken, extracts the mood, and generates follow-up questions where applicable.

Speech recognition is processed within the Google Cloud EU multi-region. Text structuring takes place in the europe-west3 region (Frankfurt am Main). Your content is not used to train public AI models.

7. Recipients / Processors

To provide the App, we use carefully selected service providers with whom data processing agreements (Art. 28 GDPR) are in place:

  • Google Cloud Run – backend processing (in-memory) · Google · europe-west3
  • Firebase – authentication, database, synchronization · Google · europe-west3
  • Speech-to-Text (Chirp) – audio transcription · Google · EU
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash (Vertex AI) – text structuring & analysis · Google · EU
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – subscription and payment processing · Apple / Google

The provider in each case is Google Ireland Limited or, where applicable, Google LLC. Where a transfer to third countries occurs, it is safeguarded by appropriate guarantees (in particular EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Legal Bases

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of the usage contract (provision of App functions, account, subscription).
  • Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR – explicit consent for diary content requiring special protection.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest (e.g. stability, error analysis, security).
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR – compliance with legal obligations.

9. Storage Location and Retention Period

Your diary entries and account data are stored in the EU (Frankfurt, europe-west3). In addition, entries are stored locally on your device (SQLite database); this data leaves your device only for synchronization with the cloud. We store your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete individual entries or your entire account, the associated data is deleted. In line with the Zero-Disk Policy, audio recordings are never permanently stored in the first place.

10. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access to the data processed (Art. 15)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”, Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability – export of your entries (Art. 20)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21)
  • Withdrawal of consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

To exercise your rights, a message to blaichmathias@gmail.com is sufficient.

11. Data Security

We protect your data through technical and organizational measures. Data is transmitted in encrypted form (TLS), and data at rest is stored encrypted (AES-256). Access to processing systems is restricted to what is necessary.

12. Authentication

For sign-in and account management, we use Firebase Authentication. In doing so, your account identifier and authentication data are processed in order to give you secure access to your entries.

13. Subscriptions and Payments

Paid features are offered as a subscription. Purchase and billing are handled exclusively via the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive complete payment data (e.g. credit card numbers), only information about the status of your subscription. The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply in addition.

14. Minors

The App is not directed at children. Persons under 16 years of age may only use the App with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.
