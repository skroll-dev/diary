# Promotional Text

Just tap and talk. My AI-Diary turns your spoken thoughts into a finished diary entry – with mood tags and gentle follow-up questions. Private and hosted in the EU.


# Description

Speak freely. Get a finished diary entry.

My AI-Diary turns what you say into a clear, structured diary entry – no typing required. Tap once, talk about your day the way you would to a friend, and the AI does the rest.

HOW IT WORKS

• One-tap recording
Press the button and start talking. No prompts, no forms, no blank page. Prefer typing? That works too.

• Smart structuring
The AI removes filler words, sorts your thoughts into topics and writes a polished entry in the first person – in your words, only clearer. Every topic keeps its full text; nothing is cut short.

• Automatic mood tagging
Each entry is tagged with your mood, so you can see at a glance how your days felt.

• Follow-up questions
Thoughtful questions help you reflect a little deeper on your day. Answer them by voice whenever you like – the AI weaves your answer into the right topic.

• Add to your day
Remembered something later? Record again and the AI merges it into today's entry instead of starting a new one.

• Photos
Attach photos to your entries. Location and all other metadata are removed before anything is stored.

• Your diary at a glance
Browse past days in a calm, book-like timeline and jump quickly to any month or year.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

No AI coach interrupting you mid-thought, and no empty journal that demands discipline. You simply talk – the structure comes afterwards. Warm, unhurried and effortless.

PRIVACY FIRST

• Hosted in the EU: your data is processed and stored on servers in the European Union.
• Audio is never stored: recordings are processed in memory only and discarded right after transcription.
• Encrypted: your entries are encrypted in transit and at rest.
• You stay in control: export all your data or delete your account and everything in it – right from the app.

Available in English and German.

Your day, in your words – only clearer.
