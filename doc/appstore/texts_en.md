# LONG

Speak, the AI writes.

My AI Journal turns a single, unstructured spoken thought into a structured, insightful journal entry – with zero typing required. Just tap once, speak freely for 60 seconds, and you’re done.

HOW IT WORKS

One-Tap Recording: Press the button and simply start talking.

Smart Structuring: The AI organizes your thoughts, smooths out filler words, and crafts a polished entry written in the first person.

Automatic Mood Tagging: Your mood is automatically detected and tagged.

Targeted Reflection Prompts: Specific follow-up questions help you reflect deeper on individual days – and if you like, you can answer them by voice as well.

WHY IT’S DIFFERENT
No annoying interruptions mid-thought like with conventional AI coaches, and no empty archive demanding strict discipline. You speak freely – the AI structures everything afterward. Asynchronous, warm, and frictionless.

PRIVACY FIRST

GDPR-Compliant Hosting: Hosted in Frankfurt (Google Cloud, europe-west3 region).

Zero-Disk Policy: Your audio is processed exclusively in-memory (RAM) and immediately erased after analysis. It is never permanently stored on servers.

End-to-End Security: Encrypted transmission and storage for all your entries.
